Clarifications & Logistics

FAQs about SAFAR!

What is SAFAR? ⌄
A bespoke expedition and luxury travel concierge for Pakistan. We curate premium journeys across the high glacial valleys of Gilgit-Baltistan, ancient Indus civilizations in Sindh, and coastal routes in Balochistan with verified 4x4 Land Cruisers, licensed local guides, and luxury heritage stays.
Are domestic flights to Skardu and Gilgit included? ⌄
Yes, round-trip flights from Islamabad can be included. Because northern mountain weather can occasionally delay flights, SAFAR maintains an immediate standby 4x4 Land Cruiser backup fleet via the Karakoram Highway so your trip itinerary proceeds uninterrupted.
How do I book a private or custom family tour? ⌄
Simple and friction-free. Click "Plan Your Journey" to specify your dates, group size, and destination preference. A dedicated destination coordinator in Karachi or Islamabad will provide an itemized proposal via WhatsApp within 24 hours.
What is your weather cancellation & refund policy? ⌄
If landslides or severe weather close high mountain passes, our ground team immediately re-routes your group to safe, luxury alternative valleys (such as Serena Shigar or Serena Hunza) or provides a 100% travel credit valid for 12 months.
Do foreign tourists or overseas Pakistanis require special permits? ⌄
Most top destinations—including Hunza, Skardu, Swat, Thatta, and Mohenjo-daro—do not require special NOC permits for international travelers. For restricted border regions or high-altitude climbing zones, SAFAR processes all government security clearances on your behalf.
